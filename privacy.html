Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

CreatorFlow AI ("the app") is operated by MD Jisan Sarkar for managing his own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data we access

When you connect a YouTube channel, the app uses YouTube API Services to access: your channel information, your videos and their public statistics, YouTube Analytics reports for your own content, and comments on your videos. When you connect Facebook or Instagram, the app accesses the Pages and professional accounts you authorise, their posts, insights and comments.

How we use it

  • To upload and schedule content you create and approve.
  • To show you analytics and performance reports about your own content.
  • To classify comments and draft replies, which are posted only with your approval or under settings you enable.

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted (AES-GCM) on the owner's own computer. Analytics and content data are stored locally in the app's database. Passwords are never requested or stored.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

You can disconnect any account inside the app, and revoke access at any time from your Google account's security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions). On disconnection, stored tokens are deleted; you can request deletion of all stored data by emailing the contact below.
