CreatorFlow AI

CreatorFlow AI is a private social media management tool operated by MD Jisan Sarkar. It helps the owner plan, create, schedule and analyse content for their own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts.

The tool connects to these platforms only through their official sign-in (OAuth) and APIs. It never asks for or stores passwords.

What it does with your Google account

  • Uploads and schedules videos you created to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Reads your channel's videos, statistics and comments to show analytics and draft replies (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Posts replies to comments only when you approve them (youtube.force-ssl).
